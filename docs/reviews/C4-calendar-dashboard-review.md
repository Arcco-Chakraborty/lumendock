## Review evidence

Inspected the C4 brief, approved design spec and implementation plan, frozen C1 schemas, C4 implementation report, snapshot-relative diff (`task-4-diff.md`), Playwright screenshot evidence in `dashboard/evidence/calendar/`, and current dashboard source and test files with line numbers:

- `dashboard/src/calendar/time.ts` (`1–246`):
  - **Pure UTC Gregorian date arithmetic (`time.ts:16–31`):** `addDays` parses `YYYY-MM-DD` and steps dates via `Date.UTC(year, month - 1, day + deltaDays)` and `getUTCFullYear`/`getUTCMonth`/`getUTCDate`, avoiding local-time `24 * 3_600_000` drift across DST transitions such as `2026-03-08` in `America/New_York`.
  - **Exclusive all-day ends and timezone-aware spans (`time.ts:33–147, 180–202`):** `eventDates` subtracts 1 day from all-day `end.date` (`addDays(event.end.date, -1)`) so `end.date` remains exclusive; `isToday` checks inclusive `[first, last]` date bounds; timed instants format in the requested IANA display time zone via `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, and `eventDetailWhen` appends `(source: ${event.start.timeZone})` when the event's source zone differs from the display zone.
  - **Advancing `nextSummary` and duplicate source disambiguation (`time.ts:204–246`):** `nextSummary` rejects all-day (`kind !== "time"`), self-declined (`selfResponseStatus === "declined"`), and ended (`end.epochMs <= nowEpochMs`) events, renders `Now · until ...` when `start.epochMs <= nowEpochMs < end.epochMs`, and renders `Starts ... · until ...` for upcoming timed events. `sourceDisplayName` appends `(${source.sourceId.slice(-6)})` whenever multiple sources share the same display name.

- `dashboard/src/calendar/store.ts` (`1–635`):
  - **Tab-scoped and visibility-scoped polling (`store.ts:166–176, 453–482, 629–634`):** `CalendarStore` starts with `active = false` and polls `hub.calendar` only while `this.active && this.visible && !this.disposed && this.adapter` (`2000 ms` when `state === "loading"`, `5000 ms` otherwise). Switching away from Agenda, hiding the document, or calling `dispose()` immediately cancels timers while preserving in-memory session data across tab switches.
  - **Monotonic offline clock and staleness advancement (`store.ts:113–164, 484–504`):** `recordTiming` anchors `serverNowEpochMs + (ageMs ?? 0)` against `clock.now()`. `snapshot()` advances `nowEpochMs` and `ageMs` (`syncAgeMs`) using elapsed monotonic time so ongoing/ended `nextEvent` status and the staleness threshold (`STALE_THRESHOLD_MS = 180_000`) advance even when USB/laptop connectivity is lost.
  - **Session cache preservation vs. privacy purge (`store.ts:380–405, 506–525, 577–586`):** Ordinary USB/bridge/hub connectivity failures (`USB_UNAVAILABLE`, `HUB_UNAVAILABLE`, `REQUEST_TIMEOUT`, `BRIDGE_UNAVAILABLE`) set `offline = true` and `linkStale = true` while retaining cached `items` and `page`. Conversely, `CALENDAR_UNSUPPORTED`, `AUTH_REQUIRED`, `state === "disabled"`, and `state === "setup_required"` immediately purge `items`, `nextEvent`, and `nextCursor`; `selectSources()` immediately filters out deselected sources from `items` and `page.nextEvent`.
  - **Pagination, cursor-expiry recovery, and race protection (`store.ts:178–311, 527–627`):** `setRange`, `refresh`, and `selectSources` increment `this.generation` so late in-flight responses are ignored (`reqGen !== this.generation`), and `readPage` additionally verifies `data.range` equality and `requiredSelectionRevision`. `loadMore()` appends deduplicated items (`${item.sourceId}:${item.id}`), merges `pageSources`, and automatically clears `nextCursor` and restarts from page 1 (`readPage(false)`) on `CALENDAR_CURSOR_EXPIRED`, `CALENDAR_SELECTION_CHANGED`, or a mid-pagination `revision`/`selectionRevision` change. When extra pages are loaded (`hasLoadedMore`), background polls within the same `revision` and `selectionRevision` update page metadata without dropping pages > 1 (`store.ts:590–600`).
  - **Multi-page source enumeration and explicit selection (`store.ts:313–451`):** `loadSources()` pages through `hub.calendarSources` until `nextCursor === null` (restarting cleanly once if `selectionRevision` changes mid-enumeration), and `selectSources()` submits unique `sourceIds` (max 100) with `expectedRevision` to `hub.calendarSelect`.
